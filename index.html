What you get, step by step

Follow one job from the first call to the money in the bank

This is everything your subscription covers, in the order a job happens. Every step is a screen in the app, and everyone sees where each job stands: the office, the salespeople, the store and the team on site.

1Office & salespeople

An enquiry comes in

Every enquiry goes into one list with its next step: call back, send a quote or book a visit. Your salespeople get the same list on their phone.

  • The next step and date on every enquiry
  • Reminders on the phone before each appointment
  • A sales app for your salespeople to log new sales and documents
2Salesperson or office

The site visit

Book the visit, go and measure, then mark it done with your notes. Pending and done visits sit on one screen, so none are forgotten.

  • Visit date and status for every client
  • Measurements and access notes saved to the client
  • Photos and drawings kept with the visit
3Office

Turn the visit into a job

One tap creates the job. Pick the team, the date and the materials, and choose the items it needs from your store.

  • See how many of each item are available as you choose
  • Store items are kept aside for this job the moment it's created
  • The job appears on the installations calendar and the team's phone
4Store keeper

The store gets the materials ready

Your store keeper has their own app with the day's deliveries. They scan each item out as it leaves, so you always know what went to which client.

  • A delivery list for each job, by date and team
  • Every item scanned out with a handheld scanner or the phone camera
  • Every serial number linked to the client, ready for warranty
5Installer team

The team does the job on their phone

Each team sees only its own jobs: the address with directions, the work to do, the materials and any note from the office. They take photos in the boxes you set, then press Job done.

  • Photo boxes you choose for each type of work, with no limit on photos
  • A job can't be finished without photos
  • The office gets an alert when the job is done
6Office

You check the photos and close the job

Open the alert and look at the photos of each stage. If you're happy, press Installed and the job is closed.

  • Photos sorted by stage: before, during and finished
  • Download all the photos of a job in one file for warranty or disputes
  • Every installed job kept with its client
7Office

Collect the money

Once the job is installed, the balance moves to Money to collect. You see who still owes you and how much, without digging through files.

  • Every balance still owed, in one list
  • One tap sends a polite WhatsApp reminder
  • Your bank details included in the reminder
The Store

Know what's in stock, what's kept for jobs and what to order

Behind every job is your store. SiteToFit keeps it up to date as items come in and go out, so a team never turns up short.

Stock in

Scan items in as they arrive. Appliances are scanned one by one by their serial barcode. For fixings like hinges, scan the box and type how many.

Stock out

Each job's materials wait in a delivery list by date and team. On the day, the store keeper scans every item out, and each serial number is linked to the client it went to.

Stock

For every product: how many are in the store, how many are kept for jobs, and how many are free to use. Type or scan a serial number to see where it went.

To order

What to order so every booked job is covered, and which job would be short without it. Items below your warning level are flagged too.

Also in the StoreStock take: scan every shelf and see what's missing. Notes for the store keeper.
