Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data SiteToFit handles, why, where it is kept and the rights you have. SiteToFit is an app for companies that sell, measure and install, and this website at siteto.fit.

Who we are

SiteToFit, run by Gary Falzon, Malta

Email: gary.falzon2104@gmail.com

Phone & WhatsApp: +356 7932 3756

Two different roles

  • Data your company puts into SiteToFit: your clients' names, addresses and phone numbers, site visit notes, job photos, stock and payments. Your company decides what goes in and why, so your company is the controller of that data. SiteToFit stores and processes it only to run the app for you, as your processor, and never uses it for anything else.
  • Data about you as a user or contact of ours: your login, your name and role, and any messages you send us. For this, SiteToFit is the controller.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, role (for example office, salesperson, store keeper or installer team) and which company you belong to.
  • Business data your company adds: clients, enquiries, site visits, jobs, photos, stock, serial numbers and balances owed.
  • Basic technical data needed to keep the app working and secure, such as when you last signed in.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, email or phone, including feedback on the demo. The website and demo do not store your answers; they only open WhatsApp or your email with the text ready for you to send.

We do not sell personal data, show advertising or use your data to build marketing profiles.

Why we use it

  • To provide the app your company has signed up for (performing our contract).
  • To keep accounts secure, fix problems and give support (legitimate interests).
  • To keep records the law requires, such as invoices (legal obligation).

Where it is kept and who helps us

  • App data, logins and photos are stored with Google Firebase in the European Union (Belgium). Google acts as our sub-processor under its data processing terms.
  • This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The website itself does not collect personal data.
  • The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google and shares your IP address with it.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

How long we keep it

We keep your company's data for as long as your company uses SiteToFit. When a subscription ends, your company can ask for an export within 30 days. After that we delete the data within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep part of it longer, such as invoices.

How we protect it

  • Each company's data is kept separate. People can only see data from their own company, and only the parts their role needs.
  • Data travels over encrypted connections (https) and is encrypted where it is stored.
  • Access is controlled by security rules on our servers, not only by what the app shows on screen.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit or object to how we use it, or receive a copy to take elsewhere. Contact us using the details above and we will reply within one month.

If your request is about data your company added to SiteToFit, such as you being one of its clients, we will pass it to that company, because it decides how that data is used.

You can also complain to the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) in Malta.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The app keeps small items in your browser that are needed to keep you signed in. These are strictly necessary, so no consent banner is needed.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your company's data is used, we will tell your company before it takes effect.
